Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information on reporting cyber incidents, evidence preservation, and officer workflows.

⏳ What is the "Golden Hour" rule?

Reporting financial fraud immediately to Helpline 1930 enables officers to dispatch auto-freeze requests to recipient bank nodes before funds are laundered.

📄 What digital evidence can I upload?

You can upload payment receipts (PDF), bank statements, WhatsApp/SMS chat screenshots (JPG/PNG), audio call logs, and screen recordings (MP4).

🔐 How is my session authenticated?

Victims log in using the Case Registration Number and a 6-digit OTP code. A 24-hour encrypted JWT token grants access to your case portal.

🕵️‍♂️ How do officers handle my case?

Assigned cyber personnel review your submitted evidence, update priority severity, post audit remarks, and forward case instructions directly to your status log.
